## 5. AEO service — $49.99/month — "Be found"

Approved description: Customers increasingly use search and AI to decide who to
call, visit and trust. FrontDesk helps a business become easier for those
systems to understand, discover, and appropriately represent.

AEO is a continuous loop, not a one-time audit:
**Scan → Understand → Identify opportunity → Generate fix → Validate → Publish → Measure → Repeat**

- **Scan:** examines the business's digital presence (website structure,
  business information, services, locations, content, question coverage,
  AI-search signals).
- **Understand:** builds a structured model of the business — identity,
  services, who it serves, what customers ask, geography, competitive context.
- **Identify opportunities:** finds specific gaps (unclear service
  descriptions, missing FAQs, weak question coverage, incomplete business
  information, poor entity clarity) and explains what's missing and why it
  matters — not just a score.
- **Generate the fix:** produces actual improvements — copy, FAQ content,
  service-page recommendations, structured-data and entity fixes.
- **Validate:** checks changes against real business facts before anything
  ships. AEO never invents services, prices or claims.
- **Publish (with authorization):** where the customer authorizes access,
  FrontDesk can implement approved changes directly; otherwise it generates the
  changes for the customer or their developer to implement.
- **Measure and monitor:** tracks visibility, mentions and citations over time
  and keeps finding the next opportunity.
- Works alongside existing SEO rather than replacing it.

**What it cannot / does not do — hard boundary, always applies:**
FrontDesk does not control and cannot guarantee how ChatGPT, Google, Gemini, or
any other system ranks or recommends a business. No guaranteed rankings, no
guaranteed AI recommendations. The honest claim: FrontDesk makes a business
easier for these systems to understand and represent accurately — not a
promise of higher rank or more mentions.
