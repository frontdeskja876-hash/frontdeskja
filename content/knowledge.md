## Company

- FrontDesk JA is a technology company based in Jamaica, building AI-powered
  capabilities that help businesses handle the work that happens before, between, and
  around the job.
- Tagline: "Empowering the world with AI." Headline idea: "The front desk of the future."
  Closing line: "Be found. Be available. No hold time."
- Belief: the modern front desk is no longer limited to a person behind a desk or
  answering a phone. It is the system that helps a business get discovered, stay
  available, respond to customers, and move conversations toward real work.
- Three capabilities: Be found (AEO), Be available (Assistant), No hold time (Receptionist,
  an AI voice front desk).
- The first layer is AEO: before a customer can contact a business, they have to find
  it. From there, FrontDesk helps businesses stay available when customers reach out,
  through digital conversations, bookings, and voice.
- "We are not building AI for the sake of adding AI to a business. We are building
  technology that takes useful work off the business owner's plate": less time answering
  the same questions, less time on repetitive front-desk tasks, less time worrying
  whether customers can find or reach the business; more time doing the work that
  actually moves the business forward.
- FrontDesk JA uses its own product: the Ask FrontDesk chat on this website is a live
  instance of the FrontDesk Assistant, configured with FrontDesk JA's own public business

## AEO — $49.99/month — "Be found"

Customers increasingly use search and AI to decide who to call, visit and trust.
FrontDesk helps a business become easier for those systems to understand, discover and
appropriately represent.

AEO is a continuous loop, not a one-time audit: Scan → Understand → Identify
opportunity → Generate fix → Validate → Publish → Measure → Repeat.

- Scan: examines the business's digital presence (website structure, business
  information, services, locations, content, question coverage, AI-search signals).
- Understand: builds a structured model of the business: identity, services, who it
  serves, what customers ask, geography and competitive context.
- Identify opportunities: finds specific gaps such as unclear service descriptions,
  missing FAQs, weak question coverage, incomplete business information, poor entity
  clarity. It explains what is missing and why it matters, not just a score.
- Generate the fix: produces actual improvements: copy, FAQ content, service-page
  recommendations, structured-data and entity fixes.
- Validate: checks changes against real business facts before anything ships. AEO never
  invents services, prices or claims.
- Publish (with authorization): where the customer authorizes access, FrontDesk can
  implement approved changes directly. Otherwise it generates the changes for the
  customer or their developer to implement.
- Measure and monitor: tracks visibility, mentions and citations over time and keeps
  finding the next opportunity.
- Works alongside existing SEO rather than replacing it.

Boundary (always applies): FrontDesk does not control and cannot guarantee how ChatGPT,
Google, Gemini or any other system ranks or recommends a business. No guaranteed
rankings, no guaranteed AI recommendations. The honest claim: FrontDesk makes a business
easier for these systems to understand and represent accurately.

## Assistant — $149.99/month — "Be available"

Your business's AI digital front desk. Available 24/7. Talking to customers, answering
business questions, collecting customer information, qualifying requests, and helping
customers book.

Included:
- Business-specific configuration: it learns the business, not a generic script
- Website communication, with WhatsApp where configured
- 24/7 customer conversations, unlimited from the customer's side
- Answers to business questions and customer inquiries, from approved business information
- Lead and request qualification
- Structured customer-information capture
- Booking: connect an existing calendar or booking system, or use FrontDesk's own booking
- Configurable business rules
- Escalation to a person when needed
- Testing and deployment before go-live
- Assistant Office included at no separate charge: the client portal for monitoring and
  managing the Assistant (dashboard and status, conversation review, inquiries, leads,
  bookings, escalations, captured customer info, usage monitoring, knowledge management,
  behavior settings, booking settings, channel management, reports, billing)

## Receptionist — $199.99/month — "No hold time"

Phone only: a standalone live inbound-voice product with its own capabilities. It is not
the Assistant plus voice and does not include the Assistant's website chat. Someone who
wants both website chat and phone answering would take both packages.

No hold time, ever. Every caller gets answered, simultaneously, without waiting in a
queue.

- Inbound call answering with business-specific receptionist configuration
- Caller understanding and qualification
- Common question handling
- Call routing
- Escalation and warm transfer to a person
- Simultaneous calls: no single-caller bottleneck, no busy signal, no voicemail dead end
- Inbound calls only (no outbound dialing)
- Testing and deployment before go-live
- Receptionist Office included at no separate charge: voice-centered portal to monitor
  live activity, review calls and call records, reports, voice-minute usage, bookings,
  escalations, captured customer info, performance, configuration, billing
