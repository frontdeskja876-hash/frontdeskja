Pricing

One front desk. Three ways in.

Each package is a standalone monthly price, with no setup or deployment fee. Pick what your business needs now and add more later.

Be found

AEO

$49.99/month

Be found. Your customers are increasingly using search and AI to decide who to call, who to visit, and who to trust. FrontDesk helps your business become easier for those systems to understand, discover, and appropriately represent.

  1. Scan
  2. Understand
  3. Identify
  4. Fix
  5. Validate
  6. Publish
  7. Measure
  8. Repeat
  • Scan your website, business information, services, locations, content and question coverage
  • Understand your business: what you do, who you serve, what customers ask, and where
  • Identify specific gaps, explained by what’s missing and why it matters
  • Generate the fix: copy, FAQs, service-page recommendations, structured-data fixes
  • Validate against your real business facts. AEO never invents services, prices or claims
  • Publish approved changes where you authorize access, or hand them to you or your developer
  • Measure visibility, mentions and citations over time, then find the next opportunity
  • Works alongside your existing SEO

No one can guarantee how Google, ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI system ranks or recommends a business. AEO makes your business easier for them to understand and represent accurately.

Be available

Assistant

$149.99/month

Your business’s AI digital front desk. Available 24/7. Talking to customers, answering business questions, collecting customer information, qualifying requests, and helping customers book.

  • Configured for your business: it learns your business, not a generic script
  • Website conversations, with WhatsApp where configured
  • 24/7 customer conversations, unlimited from your customers’ side
  • Answers from your approved business information
  • Lead and request qualification
  • Structured customer-information capture
  • Booking: connect your calendar or booking system, or use FrontDesk’s own
  • Configurable business rules
  • Escalation to a person when needed
  • Testing and deployment before go-live

Assistant Office included

Your portal for the Assistant: status, conversations, inquiries, leads, bookings, escalations, captured customer info, usage, knowledge, behavior, booking and channel settings, reports and billing. No separate charge.

Want to see it work? The chat on this site is a live Assistant.

No hold time

Receptionist

$199.99/month

No hold time, ever. Every caller gets answered, simultaneously, without waiting in a queue.

  • Inbound call answering, configured for your business
  • Caller understanding and qualification
  • Common question handling
  • Call routing
  • Escalation and warm transfer to a person
  • Simultaneous calls: no busy signal, no voicemail dead end
  • Testing and deployment before go-live

Phone only: answers inbound calls (no outbound dialing).

Receptionist Office included

A voice-centered portal: live activity, calls and call records, reports, voice-minute usage, bookings, escalations, captured customer info, performance, configuration and billing. No separate charge.
